Privacy Policy

How we use the details you share with us.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry, we receive your name, mobile number and the project details you choose to share (type, locality, size, budget and timing).

Why we use it

Only to reply to your enquiry, arrange a site visit and prepare a quotation. We do not sell or share your details for others' marketing.

How long we keep it

As long as needed to respond and, if you become a client, for the project and legal record-keeping. You can ask us to delete your details at any time.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete your details, or withdraw consent, contact info@gminterior.co.in or +91 98190 30355.
