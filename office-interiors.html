Before anything is made, we draw your space to the millimetre and show you layouts, elevations and 3D views. Materials and finishes are chosen together, so there are no surprises at handover.

How we work

From site visit to handover

01

Site visit

We measure your space, listen to how you live or work, and agree a budget range. Free within Kharghar.

02

Design

Layouts, elevations and 3D views drawn to the millimetre, with materials and finishes chosen together.

03

Quotation

An itemised quote with brands, finishes and a payment schedule linked to work stages.

04

Execution

Our own carpenters and site team build it, with a supervisor on site and weekly updates.

05

Handover

A snag check together, a clean handover and a written warranty on our work.
