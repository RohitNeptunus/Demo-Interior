Home Interiors

Complete interiors for 1, 2 and 3 BHK flats and villas in Kharghar and Navi Mumbai: design, carpentry, ceilings, electricals and painting by one team.

What's included

Home interiors, turnkey from day one.

New flat owners getting possession, families upgrading, and resale buyers renovating before moving in.

  • Space planning and 3D design
  • Modular kitchen and wardrobes
  • TV units, crockery units and storage
  • False ceiling and lighting layout
  • Electrical points and fittings
  • Painting, wallpaper and wall panelling
  • Coordination of flooring and civil work
  • Deep cleaning before handover
