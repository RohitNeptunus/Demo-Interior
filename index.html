FAQ

Common questions

Do you do complete turnkey interiors?

Yes. We handle design, carpentry, false ceiling, electrical points, painting, flooring coordination and final cleaning, so you deal with one team from site visit to handover.

Which areas do you work in?

Mainly Kharghar and the rest of Navi Mumbai (Belapur, Vashi, Nerul, Ulwe, Panvel, Taloja), and selected projects in Mumbai.

How long does a 2BHK interior take?

Usually about 6 to 10 weeks after design approval, depending on scope and site readiness. You get a written schedule before work starts.

Is the site visit free?

Site visits within Kharghar are free. For other areas we confirm on call.

Do you make the furniture yourselves?

Yes. Kitchens, wardrobes and custom joinery are made by our own carpenters to the exact measurements of your space.
